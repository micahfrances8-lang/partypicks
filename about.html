🎉 What Is Party Picks?

Party Picks is a website that lets users create birthday invitations inspired by their favorite restaurant themes. Users can customize party details and preview their invitation.

Our goal is to make birthday invitation creation fun and creative while applying our knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
